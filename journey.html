Chapter 00
It Started
With Games

All my life has been about games — and games, and games. I've been deeply into video games since childhood. Playing all day and all-nighters with friends, getting lost in other worlds, obsessing over mechanics and design.

Curiosity about game development hit me in the summer of 2021. I had always dreamed of making my own cool game — and that dream is what pulled me into coding. I researched the process, the tools, the languages. That was my first day of coding.

I knew one truth immediately: I can't make a game if I can't even code. So I started there.

"I can't make a game if I can't even code. So I learned to code."

Chapter 01 – 05
The Timeline
Summer 2021 — Day One
First Line of Code
I spent that entire summer studying Java, Python, and a bit of C++. I also got my hands on the Unity Game Engine, which was genuinely exciting. I loved how the internet rewards learning — those certificates and digital badges from study platforms kept me going. Getting them felt like leveling up for real.
Java Python C++ Unity Self-Taught
2023 — 1st Year I.T Student
College, Hackathons & a Receipt System
Starting college as an I.T student, I wasn't overwhelmed — I'd already studied coding through summer. That head start let me help my colleagues with their assignments too. I built a receipt system in Java as a class project, then joined a university hackathon and placed 3rd. Competing with the brightest programmers there was a thrill, even if it was my first time on that stage.
Java Hackathon 3rd Place I.T Student
2024 — 2nd Year
Design, Mobile Dev & Attendify
This was the year of design. I made my first portfolio layouts, résumés, and UI mockups for various systems. Then we dove into mobile app development — I built Attendify, an attendance QR checker system, entirely on my own. It used APIs and Firebase as the database. I pitched it at CodeCraft, a campus mobile app convention, and it landed well with my colleagues and professors. I also visited tech companies in Cebu on an educational tour, and it genuinely shifted my perspective on this industry.
UI/UX Design Mobile Dev Firebase APIs CodeCraft Pitch Cebu Tech Tour
2025 — 3rd Year
PSITS President, Laravel & the Capstone
I became President of PSITS — the Philippine Society of Information Technology Students. Being surrounded by talented I.T people, competing in games, sports, and mind challenges, sharpened my leadership and people skills. A colleague introduced me to Laravel, and web development became my main focus. Then in summer, I started our capstone: NCIP-EAP — an educational assistance portal for Indigenous People students in Davao del Sur. It lets scholars track their status digitally, solving real problems: missing files, drenched documents, zero notifications. A system that actually matters to real people.
Laravel Web Systems PSITS President NCIP-EAP Capstone Leadership
Present Day
Internship at Infosoft
Right now I'm doing my internship at Infosoft in Matina, Davao City — putting everything I've learned into a real professional environment, building real things for real clients. The loop continues.
Infosoft Internship Matina, Davao Active

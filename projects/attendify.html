Case Study 02 // EdTech & Classroom Automation

Attendify

A real-time QR-based attendance tracking platform built to eliminate manual roll calls, prevent proxy attendance, and provide instructors with instant analytical insights.

Role
Full-Stack Web Developer
Category
EdTech & Attendance Analytics
Tech Stack
PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, QR Scanner
Deployment
Web Application
// Executive Summary

The Challenge & Vision

Taking daily attendance in large classes consumes up to 10–15 minutes of valuable instructional time per session. Paper sign-in sheets are prone to handwriting errors, lost records, and proxy signatures ("buddy signing") where absent students are marked present.

Attendify solves this by turning classroom attendance into a seamless digital experience. Instructors launch a dynamic session QR code, and students check in within seconds using their smartphone camera. The system logs timestamps, calculates attendance percentages, and generates department-ready reports automatically.

// Core Features

Capabilities & Security Architecture

📱

Contactless QR Check-In

Dynamic QR code generation per class session enabling rapid student scanning directly from screen projections or teacher devices.

🔒

Proxy Prevention Mechanism

Session-bound QR codes equipped with time-to-live (TTL) expiration and single-device verification to ensure students are physically in class.

📈

Real-Time Analytics & Trends

Dynamic charts and dashboard metrics displaying attendance rates, tardiness logs, and risk alerts for students nearing attendance thresholds.

⚡

Automated Export & Reporting

One-click export of complete attendance records into spreadsheet (CSV/Excel) and PDF formats for faculty gradebooks.

👤

Student Self-Service Dashboard

Enables students to check their personal attendance history, review excused absences, and monitor percentages across enrolled courses.

👨‍🏫

Instructor Session Controls

Allows teachers to quickly open or lock attendance sessions, override individual statuses, and add late check-in notes.

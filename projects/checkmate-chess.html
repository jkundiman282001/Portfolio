// Executive Summary

The Challenge & Vision

Traditional browser chess applications often feel either too cluttered with intrusive ads and heavy UI elements or overly bare-bones without engaging player retention mechanics. Players seeking focused practice need responsive move execution, configurable AI difficulty, and meaningful progression incentives.

Checkmate was built to deliver a modern, dark-themed competitive chess hub. It integrates the industry-standard Stockfish engine across 10 granular skill levels, provides smooth drag-and-drop piece interactions, and layers a full RPG-like progression loop — including daily quests, achievement milestones, ranked ELO tracking, and custom collectible piece/board cosmetic bundles.

// System Modules

Core Capabilities & Engine Architecture

♟️

Stockfish AI Engine Integration

Scalable AI opponent powered by Stockfish with 10 difficulty tiers — enabling beginner friendly practice up to Grandmaster-level tactical computation.

⚡

Precision Clocks & Increments

Configurable time control formats (Blitz, Rapid, Custom) with secondary Fischer increment timers (e.g. 600s + 5s increment) and active move notation logs.

🏆

Ranked ELO & Tier Progression

Dynamic ELO rating calculation tracking player skill growth across matches, complete with competitive division tiers (e.g. Gold II) and XP level advancement.

🛍️

Cosmetic Store & Loadouts

Visual-only cosmetic store featuring rarity tiers (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary) for board textures and piece skins such as Fire Spirit, Zeus, Obsidian Knights, and Royal Kingdom.

🎯

Daily Missions & Coin Rewards

Daily quest system offering gold coins and XP bonuses for completing match objectives like Warm-Up Game, Daily Victory, and Multi-Match completions.

🎖️

Trophy Achievements

Milestone tracking rewarding player breakthroughs including First Victory, Hot Streak win runs, Regular Player milestones, and the prestigious Giant Slayer badge for defeating AI level 10.

Case Study 03 // Government Scholar Management Portal

NCIP-EAP

An end-to-end scholarship management portal built for the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples — streamlining applicant filing, document verification, and educational grant disbursements.

// Executive Summary

The Challenge & Vision

The Educational Assistance Program (EAP) under the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) provides critical financial aid to students belonging to Indigenous Cultural Communities (ICCs/IPs). Processing applications manually involved mountain loads of physical paperwork across distant regional offices, resulting in delayed evaluations and disbursement backlogs.


NCIP-EAP was developed to digitize and centralize the entire scholarship workflow. Applicants can submit forms and requirement documents online, while regional administrators gain real-time tools to audit documents, verify tribal memberships, and disburse grants accurately.

// System Modules

Core Capabilities & Portal Architecture

🎓

Online Scholar Application

Accessible application portal for IP scholars with step-by-step guidance and real-time status tracking throughout evaluation phases.

📁

Document Audit & Verification

Secure digital submission and audit trail for grade slips, Certificate of Tribal Membership (CTM), and school registration proofs.

🛡️

Central Admin Dashboard

Multi-region management portal enabling officers to process applications, review qualifications, and audit scholar records efficiently.

💸

Grant Disbursement Tracker

Financial ledger module tracking scholarship fund releases, term disbursements, and payout verification across regional branches.

🔔

Status Tracking & Alerts

Automated application feedback keeping applicants and regional handlers synced on missing requirements and grant releases.

📊

Multi-Region Reporting

Automated reporting tools generating demographic distributions, scholar retention metrics, and annual funding reports for government oversight.

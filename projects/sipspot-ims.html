SIPSPOT CAFE POS & IMS

An intelligent, cloud-synced Point of Sale and Inventory Management System designed for modern coffee shops — featuring automated Bill of Materials (BOM) ingredient deduction, gross profit margin analytics, and wireless thermal receipt printing.

// Executive Summary

The Challenge & Vision

Coffee shops and food & beverage establishments operate under tight margins where food cost variance, recipe inconsistency, and manual inventory audits directly eat into profitability. Traditional off-the-shelf POS software often lacks granular ingredient-level deduction, leaving business owners blind to real-time ingredient shrinkage and actual product margins.

Sipspot Cafe POS & IMS was engineered to bridge cashier operations directly with raw warehouse inventory. When an order is placed on the register, the system dynamically calculates unit costs, applies modifier price adjustments, prints receipts via Bluetooth thermal printers, and deducts the exact grams of powder, dairy, syrups, and packaging materials from live stock in real time.

// System Modules

Core Capabilities & Architecture

☕

Touch-Optimized POS Register

Lightning-fast order entry with visual category filtering (Iced Coffee, Matcha, Cloudy Series, Frappes, Rice Bowls), multi-channel support (Dine-In, Take-Out, Delivery), and automated PWD / Senior Citizen discount calculation.

📦

Raw Stock & SKU Valuation

Centralized stock tracking for raw ingredients with real-time valuation, package-to-unit cost breakdown (₱/gram, ₱/piece), par level safety thresholds, and direct supplier management.

🧪

Recipe BOM & Margin Costing

Interactive Bill of Materials builder mapping raw ingredients to menu products. Automatically computes live Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and gross profit margins (e.g. 67.8% margin) per recipe and drink modifier.

⚠️

Par Level & Low-Stock Warnings

Automated threshold warnings that flag critical inventory levels across all connected terminals before ingredients run out during peak hours.

📈

Sales & Revenue Intelligence

Live financial dashboards displaying gross revenue, hourly sales velocity curves, average raw ingredient costs per order, and one-click CSV report exports.

🖨️

Hardware & Thermal Printing

Direct Web Bluetooth integration for 58mm/80mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printers, cash drawer kick triggers, and shift reconciliation audit trails.
